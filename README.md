# SmartStock 📦

### Intelligent Inventory & Supply Chain Management Platform

SmartStock is a modern inventory and supply chain management platform designed to help businesses organize inventory operations, manage warehouses, streamline procurement, monitor sales, and gain better visibility into their supply chain.

Built with **Flutter, Dart, and GetX**, SmartStock aims to provide a clean, responsive, and professional user experience across desktop, web, tablet, and mobile platforms.

## ✨ Key Modules

- **Dashboard:** Business overview, inventory statistics, KPIs, and analytics.
- **Inventory Management:** Product management, categories, stock levels, and movement history.
- **Warehouse Management:** Warehouse operations and location hierarchy.
- **Stock Transfers:** Warehouse-to-warehouse transfer management.
- **Supplier Management:** Supplier directory and performance overview.
- **Procurement:** Purchase requests, purchase orders, and goods receiving.
- **Sales Management:** Customer management, sales orders, and stock reservation.
- **Order Fulfillment:** Picking, packing, dispatch, and returns.
- **Inventory Intelligence:** Demand forecasting, stock-out risk, and smart reorder insights.
- **Reports & Analytics:** Inventory, sales, procurement, and warehouse reports.
- **Administration:** User management, roles, permissions, and audit logs.
